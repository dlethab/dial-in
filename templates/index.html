How to Play

Decode the secret phone number into a 10-letter word or phrase. You have 3 tries.

  • Each digit matches the letters on a classic phone keypad.
  • Tap a key repeatedly to cycle its letters, just like texting on an old phone.
  • 0 is a space between words.
  • Correct letters turn green.
  • Solve it in the fewest guesses and fastest time, then share your score.
